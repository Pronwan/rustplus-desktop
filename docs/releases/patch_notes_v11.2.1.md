# 🚀 Rust+ Desktop v11.2.1 Hotfix — Display-Frame Map Animations & Inertia

Hey @everyone! 🎉

We've rolled out a follow-up performance hotfix, **v11.2.1**, perfecting map physics and animation smoothness across all monitor refresh rates!

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Enhancements

#### ✈️ Display-Synchronized Fly-To Transitions
* **Fluid Camera Glides**: Quick-travel and fly-to animations (such as focusing on teammates, monuments, or event markers) now render directly on display refresh frames, eliminating animation jitter.

#### 🔍 Silky-Smooth Zoom Easing & Spring-Back
* **Refresh-Rate Zoom Physics**: Zoom momentum, easing curves, and bounce spring-back now track your display's native refresh rate for buttery-smooth responsiveness.

#### 🌊 Inertial Pan & Momentum Physics
* **Smooth Pan Momentum**: Pan flick inertia and boundary spring-back physics now glide without micro-stutters, matching your high-refresh monitor flawlessly.
