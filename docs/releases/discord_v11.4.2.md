# 🚀 Rust+ Desktop v11.4.2 — Instant Map Previews, Clearer Connections & Server Alerts

Hey @everyone! 🎉

**v11.4.2** is out! This update brings instant cached map previews when switching servers, clearer connection mode descriptions, persistent background auto-reconnects, modded server alerts, and Discord bot delivery fixes.

---

### 🔌 Connection & Map Previews
* **Instant map previews**: When opening or switching to a server, your saved map appears immediately as a preview while devices connect and the wipe is checked.
* **Clearer connection modes**: The connection card now clearly explains each mode — **Devices only** (devices, team status & chat without live map updates) vs **Full connect** (adds live map streaming).
* **Continuous background reconnects**: When a connection drops or map polling detects a dead socket, the app keeps retrying in the background (first every few seconds, then once a minute) without needing manual intervention.
* **Smoother loading**: Device progress bars show only when device work is actually happening, and maps generated by older parsers reparse automatically.

### 🚨 Modded Server Alerts & Discord Bot
* **Modded Server Alerts**: Broadcast alerts sent by modded servers (e.g. "Wall destroyed at R9") now have their own setting under **Settings > Alerts**, synced across the web and Cloud 24/7. Choose between **Raid alert** (alarm popup, raid channel, phone call), **Standard alert** (events channel notice, no call), or disabled.
* **Dedicated Discord channel (Premium)**: In your Discord Bot settings, you can now designate a specific channel for server alerts, or let them fall back to your raid/events channel.
* **Reliable Discord delivery (Premium)**: Fixed an issue where raid alerts didn't reach your Discord bot if a teammate on the free tier had their app open while yours was closed.

### 💬 Team Chat & Events
* **`!oilrig` unlock time**: `!oilrig` in team chat now reports how long ago the crate unlocked (e.g. *"Oil Rig crate unlocked 4m ago"*).
* **Persistent crate timer**: Oil rig crate timers survive reconnects to the same server.
